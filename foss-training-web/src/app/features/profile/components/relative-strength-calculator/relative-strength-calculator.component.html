About Strength Scoring

DOTS Formula

The DOTS formula is the modern international powerlifting standard for comparing lifters of different bodyweights. It uses a 4th-degree polynomial to balance biological scaling advantages between lighter and heavier athletes.

Wilks Formula

The classic Wilks coefficient relies on a 5th-degree polynomial. While historically ubiquitous, DOTS provides better fairness at the extreme weight classes.
